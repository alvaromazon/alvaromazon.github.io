Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are

Quiz Football Logo ("the app") is published by Quiz Football Logo: Guess Club ("we", "us"). You can contact us at alvaromazon69@gmail.com.

2. Summary

The app is a football badge quiz. It does not require an account and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress is stored on your device. The app uses Google services for ads, analytics, leaderboards and purchases, which process some data as described below.

3. Information processed

  • On your device: game progress, wildcards, language and settings. This is not sent to us and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): app instance identifier, device model and operating system, app version, language, approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address) and usage events (such as screens viewed and levels played). We use it to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, approximate location and interactions with ads. It is used to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice; you can change it later with the privacy options button in the app (shown where required).
  • Google Play Games (optional): if you sign in, Google processes your player ID, name and avatar to provide leaderboards and cloud save.
  • Purchases: handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details, only confirmation that a purchase was completed.
  • Notifications (optional): if you allow them, the app schedules a local reminder when your wildcards recharge. No data is sent for this.
  • Ratings: the app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review window.

4. Why we use it and legal basis

To run the game, show ads, measure usage and process purchases. Where the law requires it, we rely on your consent (advertising and analytics identifiers); otherwise on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed by Google as our service provider and, for advertising, by Google and its advertising partners. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

6. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than yours, under the safeguards described in its policies.

7. Retention

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics and advertising data are kept by Google according to its retention settings.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ads and analytics consent with the privacy options button in the app (where shown).
  • Limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings.
  • Manage Google Play Games data in the Google Play Games settings.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Brazil or another region with data protection laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent. Write to us and we will reply within the legal time limit. Because the app uses anonymous identifiers, we may ask you for the device identifier to locate your data. You may also complain to your data protection authority (for example, the AEPD in Spain or the ANPD in Brazil).

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age set by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above will change and we will publish the new version at this address.

11. Disclaimer

Quiz Football Logo is an independent, fan-made game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any club, league, federation or governing body. All names and logos are the property of their respective owners and appear only to identify them in a quiz.
